# Privacy Policy for Loghy

**Last updated: 12 October 2026**

Loghy is made by Akigato, an independent developer. Contact: **aki.gato.art@gmail.com**

This policy describes exactly what Loghy does with your information. It is written to
be checkable against the app's behaviour, not to be reassuring.

---

## The short version

**Loghy does not collect your data.** There is no account, no server of ours, and no
analytics. Your ideas, notes, SWOT analyses and tasks are stored only on your phone,
and we never see them.

Two things do involve someone else, and both are described in full below:

1. **Voice typing** sends your speech to your phone's own speech service (usually
   Google) to convert it to text — the same service every other app uses for dictation.
2. **An optional AI key.** If you choose to add your own Google Gemini API key, the
   messages you send are transmitted to Google by you, under your own key.

If you never use voice typing and never add an API key, Loghy sends nothing anywhere.

---

## What Loghy stores, and where

Everything below is kept in a private database inside the app's own storage on your
device. Other apps cannot read it. It is never uploaded.

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Your profile | The name you choose, your settings, your PIN |
| Your ideas | Titles, concepts, the field you picked |
| Conversations | What you type to Loghy and what Loghy answers |
| Reality checks | SWOT analyses generated for your ideas |
| Notes vault | Your notes and up to 20 past versions of each |
| Lateral sessions | Which thinking exercises you ran |
| Hit list | Your tasks, estimates and reminder times |
| Reports | Bug reports and ideas you write for the developer |

**Uninstalling Loghy deletes all of it.** There is no cloud copy, which also means
there is nothing to restore — please export anything you want to keep.

We have also turned off Android's automatic cloud backup for this app, so your notes
are not copied to Google Drive.

---

## Your PIN

If you set an app lock PIN, it is **not stored**. Only a scrambled, one-way version
of it is kept, which can be used to check a PIN you type but cannot be turned back
into your PIN. Nobody — including us — can read it.

Please note that the app lock is a privacy convenience, not high-security protection.
It stops someone casually opening your notes. It is not designed to withstand a
determined attacker with physical access to an unlocked phone.

## Permissions, and why each one exists

| Permission | Why | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| **Microphone** | Voice typing | Only active while you hold the mic button. Your phone's speech service converts it to text — see below. |
| **Internet** | Only for the optional AI key | Unused unless you add your own key. |
| **Notifications** | Task reminders | Local reminders only. Nothing is sent anywhere. |
| **Vibration** | Tactile feedback | No data involved. |

You can refuse the microphone and notification permissions and the rest of the app
works normally.

## Voice typing — read this if you use the microphone

Loghy uses the speech-to-text service built into your Android phone. On most phones
that service is provided by Google, and **your recorded speech is sent to Google's
servers to be turned into text.** This is how dictation works in nearly every Android
app, but we want to be explicit rather than claim Loghy is "fully offline" while using
it.

- Loghy does not store your audio. We receive only the resulting text, which is then
  treated like anything you typed.
- Google's handling of that audio is covered by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **If you would rather nothing leave your device, simply type instead of dictating.**
  Some Android versions also offer on-device recognition in system settings.

---

## The optional AI key

Loghy's thinking engine runs entirely on your phone and needs no internet.

You may optionally add your own Google Gemini API key for richer answers. If you do:

- The messages you send are transmitted **to Google**, using **your** key and **your**
  account.
- Your key is stored on your device and is never transmitted to us.
- Any cost is between you and Google. We never charge you and never see your usage.
- Google's handling of that data is covered by their own terms.

**No API key is built into Loghy.** If you do not add one, no AI request is ever made.

---

## Bug reports and feedback

Reports you write are saved **on your device**. They are not uploaded automatically.

They reach the developer only if you deliberately tap **Share** or **Email Report**,
which hands the text to your own email or messaging app for you to send. You choose
what to send and to whom. If you email a report, we will see your email address and
whatever you wrote, and we use it only to fix the problem or consider the idea.

---

## What Loghy does *not* do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
- No crash reporting services
- No accounts, no sign-in, no profile on any server
- No selling or sharing of data — there is nothing to sell
- **Your ideas and writing are never used to train any AI model.** Loghy's on-device
  engine does not learn from your input; it does not change based on what you write.

## Children

Loghy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything
from them. Since the app collects nothing at all, there is nothing to delete, but you
may contact us with any concern.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, we hold nothing about you — so there is
nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf.

- **To see your data:** open the app.
- **To delete it:** delete individual notes in the app, or uninstall Loghy to remove
  everything.

If you are in the EU/UK, this means we are not holding personal data about you as a
data controller for anything stored in the app. For an email you choose to send us, we
keep it only as long as needed to act on it, and you can ask us to delete it at
aki.gato.art@gmail.com.
